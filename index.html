The idea and the stakeholder answers

Business idea (typed into “New project”)

We want a field service management platform. Dispatchers create work orders from customer requests, assign technicians based on skills and location, and track job progress on a live map. Technicians use a mobile app to view assigned jobs, update job status, capture photos and record parts used, even offline. Customers receive SMS notifications and can rate the service. Managers review dashboards with first-time fix rate and SLA compliance. The system must integrate with our SAP ERP for invoicing.

Plus one uploaded document, field-service-work-orders.md, parsed and indexed as untrusted text.

    Requirement quality, before and after

    The first rerun showed weak spots in the requirements step itself. These are the actual titles from that earlier run and from this one.

    • Complete work orders on site and capture the customer→Complete work orders on site and capture the customer signatureTitles now end on a complete phrase instead of being cut after nine words.
    • Assign work orders to technicians based on skills and→Assign work orders to technicians based on skills and availabilitySame fix. Long clauses are shortened at a natural break, never mid-phrase.
    • Order can have part · Order has a reference number · Search should be returned→Removed as FRs. Data rules go to the data model; “Search results should be returned in under 500 ms” is now a performance NFRNouns used as sentence subjects were being read as verbs.
    • 9 restated requirements, e.g. “Assign work orders”, “Capture the customer signature”→Merged into the more specific requirementNear-duplicates are detected and the most specific wording is kept.
    • Performance requirement · Availability requirement (plus generic baselines beside them)→Dispatch board shall load within 2 seconds · System must be available 99.5% of the timeNFR titles come from the statement, and a stated target replaces the generic baseline.
    • I want “to Technicians must be able to view…” · when “I Customers receive SMS notifications and can with valid information”→I want to view their assigned work orders on the mobile app · when I rate the serviceStories and Given/When/Then criteria are built from the capability, with checks that suit the action (view, export, notify, edit).
    • Discovery answers had little effect on the requirements→GDPR and SOC 2 compliance, 7-year retention and 8-hour offline work now appear as requirementsAll ten answers were entered through the UI and flow into every downstream agent.
    • Project switcher showed “Select a project” right after creating one→The new project appears in the switcher at onceFrontend fix: creating or renaming a project refreshes the project list.

    Issues still visible in this run’s output

    A review of the requirements above found these gaps. They come from the app’s requirement agents, so they need a fix in the app code and a fresh run.

    • Offline sync target conflicts after the change request. FR-007 moved to 60 seconds (v2), but related items were not updated: FR-003, RULE-003 and RULE-004 still say 2 minutes and TR-001 says 5 minutes.FR-003 · FR-007 · RULE-003 · RULE-004 · TR-001
    • Some rule titles are still cut mid-phrase. “Technicians see only their own work orders; managers see” and “The system shall validate that the site address belongs”.RULE-002 · RULE-006
    • SOC 2 requirement reuses the GDPR wording. NFR-014 lists consent and 30-day subject-access handling, which are GDPR duties, not SOC 2 controls.NFR-014
    • Duplicates are still present. Several rules repeat a functional requirement word for word, and “view assigned jobs” repeats “view their assigned work orders”.FR-003/RULE-003 · FR-010/RULE-005 · FR-013/RULE-006 · FR-005/FR-020
    • An open question was stored as a business rule. RULE-007 is a question, not a rule, and some business requirement wording is rough (“Sms Notification management”, “dispatchers, technicians to assign work orders”).RULE-007 · BR-001 · BR-006
